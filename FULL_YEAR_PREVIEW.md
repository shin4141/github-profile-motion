For `2025-10-22` and `2026-05-31`, public GraphQL reported zero while the profile tooltip showed one contribution. The renderer does not invent those counts or claim perfect identity with GitHub's separately rendered native graph. Public/private contribution visibility is a possible explanation, not established proof. The existing public-data scope and no-new-secret workflow remain unchanged.
